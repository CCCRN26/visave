# Visave User Troubleshooting Guide

## About this guide

This guide is for Visave users, group officers, facilitators, and programme staff. It explains the actions that are available in the current Visave screens and the usual reasons an action is unavailable.

Visave uses two ways of running a group:

- **Program Assisted:** the assigned active Facilitator and authorized programme staff operate the group.
- **Member Managed:** the current Chairperson and Record Keeper must have Visave logins. The Chairperson and Record Keeper perform different duties.

Never share your Visave password with support staff or the chatbot.

## Quick troubleshooting checklist

Before asking for help:

1. Confirm that you are signed in with the correct account.
2. Refresh Visave and reopen the group or meeting.
3. Check that you opened the correct group, cycle, and meeting.
4. Read the message shown on the screen and complete any earlier step that is still open.
5. If the problem continues, take a screenshot and contact the facilitator, programme administrator, or support team.

## Login and account access

### How to log in

1. Open Visave and select **Sign in**.
2. Enter your email address and password.
3. Select **Sign in**.
4. A group member is taken to **My Groups**. Other users are taken to the dashboard.

### User may ask

- Why can’t I log in?
- Visave says my email or password is invalid.
- My login is not working.

## Complaint: I cannot sign in

**Likely reason**

The email address or password may be incorrect, or the account may no longer be active. Visave gives the same sign-in message for these cases.

**What to do**

1. Re-enter the email address used for your Visave account.
2. Re-enter the password carefully.
3. Make sure you are using an active Visave account, not a group member record without digital access.
4. If it still fails, ask an authorized administrator to check whether the account is active.

**If it still does not work**

Do not send your password. Provide your email address, role, and a screenshot of the message to support.

### User may ask

- Why was I logged out?
- Visave keeps sending me back to login.
- My session expired.

## Complaint: My Visave session has ended

**Likely reason**

Visave sessions expire after a period of inactivity, and an inactive account cannot keep a session.

**What to do**

1. Return to **Sign in**.
2. Sign in again with your own email and password.
3. Reopen the group or meeting and check that the earlier action was saved before repeating it.

### How to log out

1. Select **Log out** in Visave.
2. Wait until Visave returns to the sign-in page.
3. On a shared device, close the browser when you have finished.

## Dashboard and navigation

### How to open a group

1. Facilitators and programme staff: select **Groups** or **My Groups** from the sidebar.
2. Use the search and location filters if needed.
3. Select the group code or group name.
4. Use the group tabs for **Members**, **Cycle**, **Meetings**, **Loans**, and **Reports**.

### User may ask

- Why can’t I see my group?
- My group is missing from the dashboard.
- Why can’t a facilitator open this group?

## Complaint: I cannot see or open a group

**Likely reason**

Visave limits groups by assignment and programme area. A Facilitator sees groups assigned to that Facilitator. Project and State Coordinators see only their authorized programme area. A member sees only groups linked to that member’s Visave login.

**What to do**

1. Check **My Groups** if you are signing in as a member.
2. For a Facilitator, confirm that the group is assigned to you and that your Facilitator account is active.
3. For a Project or State Coordinator, confirm that the group belongs to your authorized project and state.
4. Ask a programme administrator to correct the assignment or account link if necessary.

**If it still does not work**

Provide the group name/code, your role, and the page you used to look for the group.

### User may ask

- Why can’t I see another member’s statement?
- Why can’t an ordinary member open the group workspace?

## Complaint: I cannot access another member’s information

**Likely reason**

An ordinary digital member can use **My Activity** to see that member’s own savings, Social Fund, loans, share-out, and history. Group records and member statements are limited to authorized programme users or the current digital Chairperson/Record Keeper.

**What to do**

1. Open **My Groups** and select **View My Activity** for your own information.
2. If you are a current Chairperson or Record Keeper, make sure your group-member record is linked to the Visave account you signed in with.
3. Ask the authorized group officer, Facilitator, or programme administrator for information you are not allowed to view.

## Groups and assignments

### How to create a group

1. Select **Groups**, then **Create Group**.
2. Enter the group name, project, state, LGA, and available group details.
3. Select **Create Savings Group**.
4. Continue to onboarding to complete the group setup.

Only authorized programme users and eligible Facilitators can create groups. A Facilitator can create only inside that Facilitator’s assigned area. New Facilitator-created groups begin in Program Assisted onboarding and are not publicly listed by default.

### User may ask

- Why can’t I create a group?
- Why can’t I assign a Facilitator?
- Why is the group not on my list?

## Complaint: A group action is unavailable

**Likely reason**

The action may belong to another role, the group may be outside your assigned area, or the group may already be Member Managed.

**What to do**

1. Check that you are in the intended group and signed in with the intended role.
2. If you are a Facilitator, confirm that you are the group’s active assigned Facilitator.
3. If the group is Member Managed, ask the current Chairperson or Record Keeper to complete member-operated work.
4. Ask a programme administrator to review the assignment only if it is incorrect.

## Group setup and activation

### How to activate a group

1. Open the group and select **Continue onboarding**.
2. Complete the group profile, approved constitution, cycle schedule, members, and officers.
3. Open **Review & Activate**.
4. Resolve every blocking item shown by Visave.
5. Select **Activate group**.

### User may ask

- Why can’t I activate the group?
- Activate group button is disabled.
- Why can’t I start Cycle 1?

## Complaint: I cannot activate a group or first cycle

**Likely reason**

Visave requires a complete profile, an approved constitution, a valid cycle schedule, 15–25 active members, and exactly five different officers before activation.

**What to do**

1. In onboarding, read the blocking items under **Review & Activate**.
2. Add or correct the group profile and cycle dates.
3. Approve the constitution after its lending terms are complete.
4. Ensure there are 15–25 active members.
5. Assign one active cycle participant to each required role: Chairperson, Record Keeper, Box Keeper, Money Counter 1, and Money Counter 2.
6. Select **Activate group** again.

### User may ask

- Why can’t we switch to Member Managed?
- Why is handover blocked?

## Complaint: The group cannot become Member Managed

**Likely reason**

The group must be active with an active cycle and approved constitution. All five officers must be assigned, the Chairperson and Record Keeper must be active members with Visave logins, the assigned Facilitator must be active, and no meeting or cycle close-out can be open.

**What to do**

1. Open the group overview and review the Member Managed readiness items.
2. Complete the missing officer assignment or active-cycle requirement.
3. Enable digital access for the current Chairperson and Record Keeper.
4. Close any open meeting and complete any current cycle close-out.
5. Ask an authorized programme user to complete the operating-model handover.

### How to manage public group listing

1. Open the group overview.
2. Under **Find a Group listing**, select **Manage Public Settings**.
3. Choose whether the listing is **Hidden** or **Visible**.
4. Choose the membership-intake setting and save.

Only the assigned Facilitator or authorized programme staff can change public listing settings.

## Members

### How to add a member to an active cycle

1. Open **Members** for the group.
2. Under **Cycle Participation**, select **Add Member** or **Add Existing Member to Current Cycle**.
3. Enter the member details or select the existing member.
4. Choose an effective join date within the cycle.
5. Save the member. If appropriate, select the option to add the member to the currently open meeting.

### User may ask

- Why can’t I add a member to this cycle?
- Why is the member not available for this meeting?
- Why can’t I remove a member from the cycle?

## Complaint: A member cannot be added, removed, or used in a meeting

**Likely reason**

The member must be active and participating on the meeting date. A member can be removed from a cycle only while the next cycle is **READY**, not after it is active. An officer must be reassigned before that officer can be removed from a ready cycle.

**What to do**

1. Open **Members** and check the member’s group status and cycle participation.
2. Add the member with an effective date inside the cycle and not before the member joined the group.
3. For an open meeting, use the available option to add an eligible current-cycle participant to that meeting.
4. If removing a ready-cycle participant, first change that member’s officer assignment if one exists.

### User may ask

- Why does Visave reject the phone number?
- My member phone number will not save.

## Complaint: The phone number is invalid

**Likely reason**

Visave accepts a phone number only when it has exactly 11 digits.

**What to do**

1. Enter digits only.
2. Check that the number has exactly 11 digits.
3. Remove spaces, letters, and extra country-code digits.
4. Save the form again.

### How to enable a member’s digital access

1. Open **Members** and select the member.
2. Under **Digital Access**, select **Enable Digital Access**.
3. Enter the member’s email, optional phone, and a temporary password of at least 12 characters.
4. Select **Create Login**.
5. Give the temporary password to the member privately; the member must change it after signing in.

Only authorized programme users, or an assigned Facilitator where permitted, can manage digital access. The member must be active. Disabling access removes the link to the group member; it does not delete the user account.

## Meetings

### How to start a meeting

1. Open the group and select **Meetings**.
2. Select **Start meeting**.
3. Choose a date within the active cycle. The date cannot be in the future or before the last closed meeting.
4. Select **Start meeting**.
5. Visave creates the opening balances and attendance list automatically.

### User may ask

- Why can’t I start a meeting?
- Start meeting is missing.
- Visave says another meeting is open.

## Complaint: I cannot start a meeting

**Likely reason**

The group must be active, have an active cycle with an approved constitution, and have no other open meeting. Your role must also be allowed to operate meetings.

**What to do**

1. Check the group and cycle are **ACTIVE**.
2. Check the selected meeting date is inside the cycle and is not in the future.
3. Close or cancel the existing open meeting before starting another one.
4. If the button is unavailable, ask the authorized meeting operator for your group to start it.

### How to record attendance

1. Open the current meeting.
2. In **1. Attendance**, select each member’s status: Present, Late, Absent, or Excused.
3. Use **Mark all present** only when that is accurate.
4. Confirm that no member remains **Unmarked** before closing the meeting.

### User may ask

- Why can’t I change attendance?
- Why can’t I close the meeting with attendance done?

## Complaint: Attendance cannot be changed

**Likely reason**

Attendance becomes read-only after the meeting closes, or your role cannot operate the meeting.

**What to do**

1. Check that the meeting status is **OPEN**.
2. Confirm you are the authorized meeting operator.
3. Update the attendance status for every member before reconciliation and closure.

## Savings

### How to record savings

1. Open the current open meeting.
2. Find the member in **Contributions, Savings & Fines**.
3. Select the number of shares within the displayed allowed range.
4. Confirm the calculated amount and select **Post Savings**.
5. Check **Transaction History** to confirm that the savings are recorded.

Visave calculates the amount from the approved constitution’s share value. Savings can be recorded only for a member in that meeting snapshot, during an open meeting and active cycle.

### User may ask

- Why can’t I record savings?
- Savings button is disabled.
- Why can’t I save twice for the same member?

## Complaint: Savings cannot be recorded or recorded again

**Likely reason**

The meeting or cycle may be closed, the member may not be in the meeting, the share count may be outside the constitution range, or savings were already posted for that member in this meeting.

**What to do**

1. Confirm the meeting is open and the cycle is active.
2. Confirm the member appears in the meeting attendance list.
3. Select a number of shares within the range shown by Visave.
4. If savings are already recorded, do not post a second entry.
5. Use **Reverse Savings** while the meeting is open, then post the correct savings entry.

## Social Fund

### How to record a Social Fund contribution

1. Open the current open meeting.
2. Find the member under **Social Fund**.
3. Check the displayed required contribution.
4. Select **Record**.
5. Confirm the recorded entry in **Transaction History**.

### User may ask

- Why can’t I record Social Fund?
- Why does Visave say Social Fund is already recorded?

## Complaint: Social Fund cannot be recorded or recorded again

**Likely reason**

The contribution is set by the approved constitution. It cannot be recorded if the amount is zero, if the member is not in the meeting, if the meeting is closed, or if a contribution already exists for that member in that meeting.

**What to do**

1. Confirm the meeting is open and the member is in its attendance list.
2. Check the constitution has a Social Fund contribution greater than zero.
3. If the contribution is already recorded, use **Reverse Social Fund** while the meeting is open.
4. Record the corrected contribution only after the reversal is complete.

## Loans

### How to request a loan

1. Open the current open meeting and mark the borrower **PRESENT**.
2. In **Loans**, select the borrower.
3. Enter the requested amount, term, and a purpose of at least a few words.
4. Check the displayed maximum eligibility and term.
5. Select **Record request**.

In a Program Assisted group, authorized programme staff or the assigned Facilitator records requests. In a Member Managed group, the current digital Record Keeper records requests.

### User may ask

- Why can’t I request a loan?
- Why is this member not eligible for a loan?
- Why can’t a member borrow today?

## Complaint: A loan request cannot be recorded

**Likely reason**

The borrower must be an active participant in the cycle and marked **PRESENT** at the open meeting. The member cannot have an outstanding loan or another pending/approved request. The request must be within the constitution’s maximum amount and term.

**What to do**

1. Mark the borrower **PRESENT** in the current meeting.
2. Check the borrower is active and participating in the current cycle.
3. Check that the borrower has no outstanding loan or open request.
4. Enter an amount no higher than the displayed maximum eligibility and a term within the displayed maximum.
5. If necessary, ask the correct loan-request recorder for the group mode to enter it.

### How to approve or reject a loan

1. Open the current meeting and find the pending request in **Loans**.
2. Review the borrower’s current maximum eligibility, requested amount, term, and purpose.
3. Enter the approved amount and term, or enter a rejection note.
4. Select **Approve** or **Reject**.

### User may ask

- Why can’t the Record Keeper approve a loan?
- Why can’t I approve or reject a loan?
- Why can’t the person who recorded the request approve it?

## Complaint: I cannot approve or reject a loan

**Likely reason**

Only the current digital **Chairperson** can approve or reject a loan. The person who recorded the request cannot make its decision. An approval cannot exceed the original request, the borrower’s current eligibility, or the constitution’s maximum term.

**What to do**

1. Ask the current Chairperson to sign in with the Chairperson’s linked Visave account.
2. Make sure the request is still **PENDING** and the meeting is open.
3. Have a different authorized person record the request if the Chairperson recorded it.
4. Approve only an amount and term within the values shown in Visave, or reject with a note.

### How to disburse an approved loan

1. Keep the meeting open and find the **APPROVED** request.
2. Confirm that the borrower is still eligible and that the Savings/Loan Fund has enough cash.
3. Select **Disburse**.
4. Confirm the loan appears under **Outstanding loans**.

### User may ask

- Why can’t I disburse a loan?
- Why can’t the Chairperson disburse the loan?
- Visave says there is not enough cash to disburse.

## Complaint: An approved loan cannot be disbursed

**Likely reason**

The loan must be approved, the meeting/group/cycle must remain open and active, the borrower must still be eligible, and the Savings/Loan Fund must have enough cash. The loan approver cannot record that loan’s disbursement.

**What to do**

1. Confirm the request status is **APPROVED**.
2. Confirm the borrower has no outstanding loan and still meets the displayed eligibility.
3. Check the available Savings/Loan Fund amount.
4. Use the correct role: in a Member Managed group, the current digital Record Keeper records disbursement; in a Program Assisted group, the assigned Facilitator or authorized programme user does so.
5. If the Chairperson approved the request, ask another permitted disbursement recorder to post it.

### How to record a loan repayment

1. Open the current open meeting.
2. Under **Outstanding loans**, enter the payment amount for the loan.
3. Select **Post**.
4. Check the new outstanding balance.

The payment cannot be more than the total outstanding obligation. Visave applies the payment to service charge first and then principal.

## Transaction corrections and reversals

### How to reverse an incorrect savings or Social Fund entry

1. Open the same meeting while it is still **OPEN**.
2. Open **Transaction History**.
3. Select **Reverse Savings** or **Reverse Social Fund** for the active entry.
4. Wait for the reversed status to appear.
5. Post the correct entry if needed.

Posted financial records are not edited or deleted. Visave keeps the original record and its reversal.

### User may ask

- Why can’t I reverse a transaction?
- Why can’t I edit a posted saving?
- I posted the wrong amount. What do I do?

## Complaint: I cannot reverse or edit a transaction

**Likely reason**

Visave permits financial reversals only while the meeting and cycle are open, and only for an authorized financial operator. A reversal cannot itself be reversed, and an already reversed record cannot be reversed again.

**What to do**

1. Do not try to edit or delete the posted record.
2. Open the original open meeting and use the matching **Reverse** action.
3. After the reversal appears, post the correct transaction if appropriate.
4. If the meeting or cycle is already closed, contact the facilitator or programme administrator for review; do not bypass the close-out controls.

### User may ask

- Why can’t I reverse a loan repayment?
- Why can’t I reverse the loan disbursement?

## Complaint: A loan reversal is blocked

**Likely reason**

Loan reversals require the original open meeting and an authorized loan operator. A disbursement cannot be reversed after an active repayment. Only the latest active repayment can be reversed.

**What to do**

1. Confirm the meeting and cycle are still open.
2. Reverse later repayments first if you need to correct an earlier repayment.
3. Do not reverse a disbursement after repayments have been posted; ask support to review the correct financial sequence.
4. Use the supported reversal action, never an edit or deletion.

## Reconciliation and signatures

### How to reconcile a meeting and add the signature

1. Open the current meeting and select **Reconciliation**.
2. Count the physical Savings/Loan Fund cash and Social Fund cash separately.
3. Enter each counted amount.
4. Add the required signature in the signature area.
5. Select **Save reconciliation**.
6. Confirm both differences show zero before closing the meeting.

### User may ask

- Why is reconciliation failing?
- Why does the balance have a difference?
- Why is a signature required?

## Complaint: Reconciliation will not save or does not balance

**Likely reason**

Reconciliation is available only for an open meeting. Visave requires two separate cash counts and a PNG signature. A difference means the physical amount entered does not match Visave’s current calculated balance.

**What to do**

1. Recheck that you entered the physical cash count for the correct fund.
2. Review the meeting’s savings, Social Fund, fines, loan disbursements, and repayments.
3. Reverse and correct any wrong entry while the meeting is open.
4. Recount both cash funds and save a new signed reconciliation.
5. Do not close the meeting until both differences are zero.

**Why the signature is required**

The signature is part of the reconciliation record. Visave does not enable **Save reconciliation** until a signature has been added.

## Closing meetings

### How to close a meeting

1. Open the current meeting.
2. Mark every attendance row as Present, Late, Absent, or Excused.
3. Complete a current signed reconciliation with zero difference for both funds.
4. Select **Close meeting**.
5. Confirm that the meeting status changes to **CLOSED**.

### User may ask

- Why can’t I close the meeting?
- Meeting is not closing.
- Close meeting button is not working.
- I finished the meeting but cannot close it.

## Complaint: I cannot close the meeting

**Likely reason**

The meeting may have unmarked attendance, no reconciliation, a reconciliation difference, or a reconciliation that no longer matches the current balances after later activity. Only an authorized meeting operator can close it.

**What to do**

1. Open **Attendance** and remove every **Unmarked** status.
2. Open **Reconciliation** and confirm Savings/Loan and Social Fund differences are both zero.
3. If entries were posted after reconciliation, recount and save a fresh signed reconciliation.
4. Select **Close meeting** again.
5. If the button is unavailable, ask the authorized operator for the group mode to close it.

**If it still does not work**

Contact support with the group name, meeting number/date, your role, and the exact message shown.

## Cycles and share-out

### How to prepare and complete share-out

1. Open the cycle’s final meeting and complete attendance, financial activity, signed reconciliation, and meeting closure.
2. Open **Cycle Close-out** or **Share-out and Cycle Closure**.
3. Resolve every readiness item, including outstanding loans and unresolved loan requests.
4. Select **Prepare Share-out**.
5. The authorized approver selects **Approve Share-out**.
6. Record each member’s payout, then select **Complete share-out** when all payouts are complete.

Share-out distributes the Savings/Loan Fund based on members’ net shares. The Social Fund is not included in share-out.

### User may ask

- Why can’t I start share-out?
- Why can’t I approve share-out?
- Why can’t I complete share-out?

## Complaint: Share-out or cycle close-out is blocked

**Likely reason**

Visave blocks share-out until the final valid meeting is closed, earlier meetings are resolved, loans and pending/approved requests are resolved, at least one member has savings shares, and the financial totals agree. After share-out approval, ordinary cycle financial activity freezes.

**What to do**

1. Open **Cycle Close-out** and read every readiness item.
2. Complete or correct the final meeting first.
3. Settle outstanding loan principal and service charge, and resolve pending or approved loan requests.
4. Correct any displayed financial mismatch through the supported reversal workflow while it is still allowed.
5. Prepare, approve, and pay every share-out entitlement before completing share-out.

### User may ask

- Why can’t I carry forward Social Fund?
- Is Social Fund shared out to members?

## Complaint: The Social Fund cannot be carried forward

**Likely reason**

Social Fund is kept separate from the share-out. Carry-forward is available only after share-out is complete and a next **READY** cycle has been prepared.

**What to do**

1. Complete share-out and all member payouts.
2. Prepare the next cycle as **READY**.
3. In Cycle Close-out, select **Carry [amount] to Cycle [number]**.
4. Complete the final financial check before closing the old cycle.

### How to close a cycle and activate the next one

1. Complete share-out and Social Fund carry-forward.
2. Confirm current final balances with the required final signed reconciliation if Visave asks for it.
3. Select **Close Cycle**.
4. Select the participants for the next ready cycle.
5. Assign all five officers to the next cycle; each officer must be a participant.
6. Select **Start Cycle** or **Activate Cycle**.

### User may ask

- Why can’t I activate the next cycle?
- Why is the next cycle still READY?
- Why can’t I close the cycle?

## Complaint: I cannot close a cycle or activate the next cycle

**Likely reason**

The old cycle cannot close until share-out is complete, Social Fund is handled, closing balances are zero where required, and the final reconciliation is current and balanced. The next cycle must be **READY**, follow a closed previous cycle with completed share-out, have no unhandled Social Fund balance, have at least one participant, and have all five required officers assigned as participants.

**What to do**

1. Use **Cycle Close-out** and complete each step in order.
2. Confirm the next cycle participants.
3. Assign the Chairperson, Record Keeper, Box Keeper, Money Counter 1, and Money Counter 2 to eligible next-cycle participants.
4. Close the previous cycle.
5. Activate the ready next cycle.

## Reports

### How to view or download reports

1. Open the group and select **Reports**.
2. Select the cycle report you need.
3. Use **View Report** for the screen version.
4. Select **Download Excel** or **Download PDF** when available.
5. From a meeting, use **View Meeting Report** when the link is shown.

Reports are available for active, closing, and closed cycles. Closed cycles remain available as historical reports.

### User may ask

- Why can’t I see a report?
- Why can’t I download Excel or PDF?
- Why does Visave say report reconciliation failed?

## Complaint: A report or export is unavailable

**Likely reason**

You may not have report access, the group may not have an active/closing/closed cycle yet, or Visave found totals that do not agree and withheld the report/export.

**What to do**

1. Confirm you opened the correct group and cycle.
2. If you are a member, use **My Activity** for your own records unless you are the current digital Chairperson or Record Keeper.
3. Ask the assigned Facilitator or programme administrator for report access if your role should have it.
4. If Visave says the report reconciliation failed, do not rely on the export; contact the facilitator or administrator with the group and cycle details.

## Public Join / Find Group

### How to find a group

1. Open **Find a Group**.
2. Select the State, then the LGA.
3. Select **Find Groups**.
4. Select **View Group** to see an available listing.

Only visible, non-archived groups in active projects that are accepting requests appear in the public search.

### How to request to join a group

1. Find the group and select **Request to Join**.
2. Enter your first name, surname, and an 11-digit phone number.
3. Add an email, residence, and reason for joining if available.
4. Select **Submit Request**.
5. Keep the reference code shown by Visave.

The assigned Facilitator reviews the request. A request is not immediate membership or a Visave login.

### User may ask

- Why can’t I find my group online?
- Why can’t I submit a join request?
- Why does Visave say I already have a request?

## Complaint: I cannot find or join a public group

**Likely reason**

The group may be hidden, its intake may be closed, its project may not be active, or the group may not have an active assigned Facilitator. Visave also prevents another active request with the same phone number or email.

**What to do**

1. Check the State and LGA selections.
2. Ask the group or Facilitator whether the group is visible and accepting requests.
3. Use the same location as the group shown in Visave.
4. If you already submitted a request, keep the reference code and wait for the Facilitator’s follow-up instead of submitting duplicates.

### How to contact Visave

1. Open **Contact Us** on the public website.
2. Enter your name, optional 11-digit phone number, email, reason, and message.
3. Select **Send Message**.

## Roles and permissions

### Who can do what in Visave

- **Super Admin:** can work across the organization.
- **Project Admin:** can work within the assigned project and permitted state scope.
- **State Coordinator:** can work within the assigned state and project scope.
- **Facilitator:** can work only with groups assigned to that active Facilitator. In Program Assisted groups, the Facilitator can run setup, meetings, finance, loans, reconciliation, and close-out. In Member Managed groups, the Facilitator is a viewer/supervisor and may manage member digital access where permitted.
- **VSLA Member:** sees **My Groups** and personal activity. A member gains group workspace authority only when the active cycle links that member’s Visave account to the current Chairperson or Record Keeper role.
- **Chairperson:** a current Chairperson with a linked Visave login approves or rejects loans. That Chairperson can also operate the meeting, attendance, financial, repayment, reconciliation, and cycle-participation actions shown in Visave. In Member Managed groups, the Chairperson also has the available share-out and cycle-close-out actions.
- **Record Keeper:** in Member Managed groups, records loan requests and disbursements and can operate meetings, attendance, financial entries/reversals, repayments, and reconciliation. The Record Keeper does not approve or reject loans.
- **Box Keeper and Money Counters:** are required group officer positions, but Visave does not give them the Chairperson or Record Keeper digital operating actions merely because of those positions.

### User may ask

- Why can another officer do this but I can’t?
- Why can’t the Chairperson manage a user login?
- Why can’t the Record Keeper approve a loan?

## Complaint: A button is missing, disabled, or unavailable for my role

**Likely reason**

Visave shows or enables actions only for the applicable group role, operation mode, group assignment, and record status. A button can also disappear after a meeting/cycle closes.

**What to do**

1. Check whether the group is Program Assisted or Member Managed.
2. Check that your Chairperson or Record Keeper assignment is for the current cycle and linked to the account you used to sign in.
3. Check whether the meeting/cycle is still open.
4. Ask the correct authorized person to perform the action; do not share accounts or passwords.
5. If the role or assignment is wrong, ask a programme administrator to correct it.

## Common technical/browser problems

### User may ask

- Visave page is not loading.
- A button does nothing.
- A form will not submit.
- Visave is showing old information.

## Complaint: A page does not load or an operation fails

**Likely reason**

Your internet connection may have dropped, the session may have ended, the page may not have refreshed, or Visave may be showing a validation message for a required field.

**What to do**

1. Confirm your internet connection.
2. Refresh the page once.
3. Sign out and sign in again if Visave asks you to sign in.
4. Reopen the same group and meeting, and check whether the action was saved before trying again.
5. Read and correct the message shown on the form.
6. If the issue continues, capture the exact message and a screenshot for support.

Do not clear browser data or delete application records as a troubleshooting step.

## When to contact support

Contact the facilitator, programme administrator, or Visave support team when the normal steps in this guide do not resolve the problem, especially for a closed financial record, incorrect role assignment, missing group assignment, or a report reconciliation failure.

Provide:

- Your role.
- Group name or group code.
- Cycle and meeting number/date, when relevant.
- The action you were trying to perform.
- The exact message shown on screen.
- A screenshot, if possible.

Never share your Visave password with support staff or the chatbot. Do not send session details or financial credentials.

## Glossary

- **Active cycle:** the current cycle in which meetings and ordinary financial entries can be recorded.
- **Attendance snapshot:** the list of eligible participants created when a meeting starts.
- **Constitution:** the approved group rules that set share value, Social Fund contribution, loan limits, and other operating rules.
- **Digital access:** a Visave login linked to an active group member.
- **Member Managed:** a group mode in which the current digital Chairperson and Record Keeper operate the group in Visave.
- **Program Assisted:** a group mode in which the assigned Facilitator and authorized programme staff operate the group.
- **Reconciliation:** comparison of physical cash counts with Visave’s calculated Savings/Loan and Social Fund balances.
- **Reversal:** the supported correction that offsets a posted financial record while preserving history.
- **Share-out:** distribution of the Savings/Loan Fund to members according to net shares; it does not distribute the Social Fund.

## Items requiring staff review

- The current Visave sign-in screen does not provide a self-service password-reset option. Users who cannot sign in after checking their details need an authorized administrator to help with account access.
- Fine records can be created during a meeting, but the current meeting screen does not show a fine-reversal button. Do not tell users to edit or delete a fine; route a correction request to the authorized facilitator or programme team for review.

## Guidance for the Visave Assistant

- Ask for the user’s role when the answer depends on role or group mode.
- Ask for the group and meeting/cycle number when relevant.
- Give short numbered steps and use the screen labels in this guide.
- Never ask for passwords.
- Do not recommend database changes, edits to posted financial records, or deletion of financial history.
- Do not invent permissions or say a user can bypass a disabled action.
- For financial corrections, always direct the user to Visave’s supported reversal workflow while the meeting and cycle are open.
- Never suggest bypassing reconciliation, signatures, authorization, share-out controls, or cycle closure checks.
- If normal user actions do not resolve the issue, escalate to the facilitator, programme administrator, or support team with the relevant details.
